🐟 Eastern Sierra Trout Guide

🌈 Rainbow Trout

The most common trout in the Eastern Sierra.

Identification
  • Pink or red stripe along the side
  • Black spots on body and tail
  • Silver to olive coloration
Common Locations
  • Crowley Lake
  • Convict Lake
  • Owens River
  • Lake Sabrina
  • June Lake Loop Waters
Fun Fact

Rainbow trout are known for powerful runs and frequent jumps after being hooked.

🟤 Brown Trout

One of the most sought-after trout species in the Eastern Sierra.

Identification
  • Golden brown body
  • Black and red spots
  • Large head and broad tail
  • Few spots on the tail compared to rainbows
Common Locations
  • Owens River
  • East Walker River
  • Crowley Lake
  • Hot Creek
Fun Fact

Brown trout often become more active during early morning, evening, and low-light conditions.

🟠 Brook Trout

A favorite species found in many high-country waters.

Identification
  • Orange belly
  • Red spots with blue halos
  • White leading edge on fins
  • Worm-like markings on the back
Common Locations
  • High Sierra Lakes
  • Virginia Lakes Area
  • Saddlebag Region
  • Backcountry Streams
Fun Fact

Brook trout are actually a char rather than a true trout.

🔴 Lahontan Cutthroat Trout

The only trout native to many Eastern Sierra waters.

Identification
  • Orange-red slash under the jaw
  • Light silver or bronze coloration
  • Spots concentrated toward the tail
Common Locations
  • Select restoration fisheries
  • Certain Eastern Sierra waters
Fun Fact

Lahontan Cutthroat Trout once inhabited much of the Great Basin watershed.

🟡 California Golden Trout

California's official State Freshwater Fish.

Identification
  • Brilliant golden body
  • Orange-red stripe along the side
  • Small dark spots near the tail
  • Extremely vibrant coloration
Common Locations
  • Remote high-elevation streams
  • Golden Trout Wilderness
  • Select Eastern Sierra backcountry lakes
Fun Fact

Many anglers consider the California Golden Trout to be the most beautiful trout in North America.

🐅 Tiger Trout

A rare hybrid trout occasionally encountered in California fisheries.

Identification
  • Maze-like markings
  • Tiger-striped pattern
  • No pink stripe
Common Locations
  • Occasional specialty stocking waters
Fun Fact

Tiger Trout are a hybrid created by crossing a Brown Trout with a Brook Trout.

🎯 Quick Identification Guide

  • Pink stripe → Rainbow Trout
  • Red slash under jaw → Cutthroat Trout
  • Bright golden body → Golden Trout
  • Worm-like back markings → Brook Trout
  • Brown body with red spots → Brown Trout
  • Tiger-striping pattern → Tiger Trout
